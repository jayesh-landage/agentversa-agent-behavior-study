## 12. Lessons Learned

This scenario demonstrates that agreement on a general recommendation is not the same as a complete operational plan.

A well-supported recommendation should address:

1. What material should be disclosed and why.
2. What information must be redacted or protected.
3. How processing limits affect the proposed schedule.
4. How confidential material and witness identity concerns should be handled.
5. What further human authorization is needed.
6. What remains proposed rather than completed.

The scenario also reinforces the importance of distinguishing collective observations from evidence of an individual agent's behavior.
